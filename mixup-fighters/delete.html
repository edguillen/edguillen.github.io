Delete your Mixup Fighters data

Mixup Fighters, made by VeViGames

When you play online, the game keeps an online account for you on Google Firebase. You can delete it, and everything stored with it, at any time.

What gets deleted

  • Your online account and its random account ID
  • Your display name and player level as stored for online play
  • Your ghosts (the fighters other players fight while you are away) and the log of their fights
  • Any matchmaking entry still waiting

Nothing is kept after deletion. Match and room records are already temporary and are removed after each match.

What stays

  • Your progress, fighters and settings on your phone (uninstalling the game removes them)
  • Your purchases, which belong to your Google Play account
  • Your cloud save, which you can remove in your Google Play Games settings
  • Leaderboard scores and achievements, which belong to your Google Play Games profile

Delete in the game

  1. Open Mixup Fighters while connected to the internet.
  2. Go to Options > Data.
  3. Tap Delete online data and confirm.

Your data is deleted right away. If you open an online mode later, the game creates a new, empty online account.

Delete without the game

Email edwin.l.guillen@gmail.com with the subject "Mixup Fighters data deletion" and your Google Play Games display name. We delete your online data within 30 days and reply when it is done.
