Mixup Fighters Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the Mixup Fighters game for Android ("the game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is made by VeViGames ("we").

Information the game collects

  • Google Play Games profile. When you sign in with Google Play Games, the game receives your Play Games player ID and display name. They are used for online play, leaderboards, achievements and saving your progress to the cloud.
  • Online play data. For online matches the game stores, on Google Firebase: a random account ID, your display name, your player level, matchmaking entries, match and room records, and your "ghost" (the fighter you built and a summary of how you play, so other players can fight it while you are away). These records are used only to run online play.
  • IP address during online matches. Online matches connect your phone directly to your opponent's phone, so the two devices exchange network addresses (including IP addresses) for the length of the match.
  • Cloud save. If you are signed in, your game progress is saved to Google Play Games Saved Games so you can continue on another device.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation so the game can unlock what you bought. We never see your payment details.
  • Advertising. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about ad interactions to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Options > Data > Privacy choices in the game.

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location, and it does not include separate analytics tools. The fighters you create stay on your device unless you play online (as your ghost) or use cloud save.

Third-party services

These Google services process data as described in their own policies:

  • Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob: How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is rated for teens. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Keeping and deleting your data

Online records (queue entries, match and room records) are temporary and are removed after the match. Your account record and ghost stay until you delete them. Cloud saves can be removed from your Google Play Games settings. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device.

To delete your online data (your online account, display name, level, ghosts and their fight log), open the game and choose Options > Data > Delete online data. It is removed at once. Without the game, email us and we will delete it within 30 days. See Delete your data for both ways. Leaderboard scores and achievements belong to your Google Play Games profile and are managed in your Google account.

Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. Online records can be read and written only by signed-in players, under access rules on the server. During a live online match the two phones talk to each other directly; that connection carries the game inputs and each player's online ID, display name, level and fighter, and it is not encrypted.
